# Phillips Exeter Academy — Home

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Independent preparatory school, grades 9–12
- **What they do:** Residential and day boarding school; need-blind admissions; character + academic focus
- **Target audience:** Prospective students, families, alumni, donors
- **Page goal:** Introduce Exeter's mission, admissions pathway, campus life, and Harkness pedagogy; drive visits, applications, and giving

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Left-aligned display heading "Phillips Exeter Academy" in large sans-serif, reversed white on full-bleed photograph of yellow and red garden flowers (1.35:1 ratio). Subheading "Uniting goodness and knowledge since 1781" in near-white body serif below. No decorative badges or floating cards. Navigation sits in sticky header above: horizontal 8-link menu (About, Admissions, Academics, Student Life, Athletics, The Arts, Alumni) + pink pill-shaped "Apply" button + hamburger icon. Header background is beige. Hero background is photographic, no color overlay. Primary interaction: none in hero itself.

### Header / Navigation
Static horizontal nav: beige background, near-black text, 8 text links in sans-serif. Pink accent pill button ("Apply") sits right of links. Hamburger menu icon (menu label) on far right. Logo not present. No secondary CTA beyond Apply button.

### Primary CTA / Input
Pink pill-shaped "Apply" button: right side of header, high contrast against beige. Action: links to admissions application. Secondary CTAs in hero region: "Why Exeter?" link (serif type).

### Applying to Exeter
- **Purpose:** Frame admissions philosophy — need-blind, character-focused, financial aid messaging.
- **Layout:** h4 heading + paragraph + 1.35:1 feature image (students in outdoor setting). Text left-aligned above image, or stacked vertically.

### Our Campus
- **Purpose:** Showcase 700-acre setting, Harkness classroom infrastructure.
- **Layout:** h4 heading + paragraph + 1.35:1 feature image (campus landscape or building). Identical structure to "Applying to Exeter".

### This is Harkness
- **Purpose:** Explain Harkness pedagogy and community impact.
- **Layout:** h4 heading + paragraph + 1.35:1 feature image (classroom or discussion setting). 3-item bulleted list below: "Admission application for 2026-27 is open", "Schedule a campus visit", "Take a virtual look around" — all as inline links.

### Welcome, Principal Elliott!
- **Purpose:** Institutional leadership announcement.
- **Layout:** h2 heading + single paragraph + "Meet the Principal" link. Feature image (1.35:1) alongside text, positioned right of or below text block.

### Exeter Stories
- **Purpose:** News and student narrative aggregation.
- **Layout:** h2 heading + link to "See all the latest on Exeter Today" + 4 story cards in bento grid (mixed-span mosaic): 1 large card (story card with 1.35:1 image, headline, link) + 3 smaller cards (1 large quote card with student testimonial + attribution, 2 single-image story cards or text-heavy cards). Cards arranged: large story left (2 cols), smaller quote card center-right, 2 smaller stories below or right. Images are 1.35:1 or square (1:1) thumbnail per card type. Background alternates: beige.

### Go figure
- **Purpose:** Key metrics snapshot (financial aid, Harkness ratio, history, course count).
- **Layout:** h3 heading + 4 uniform stat cards (equal size, equal spacing). Each card: large number (near-black, sans-serif display), description text (serif body), linked action ("Financial Aid & Tuition", "Harkness", "History & Traditions", "See our Courses"). Cards in 4-column grid, center-aligned. Background: beige.

### Find yourself at Exeter × Your generosity changes lives
- **Purpose:** Dual CTA section: campus visit + giving.
- **Layout:** 2-column grid, equal width. Left column: h4 "Find yourself at Exeter" (link) + paragraph + "Visit" text/button. Right column: h4 "Your generosity changes lives" (link) + paragraph + "Give" text/button. Each column includes 1.35:1 feature image (left: prospective students indoors; right: campus building with students). Text overlays left side of each image or sits below.

### Design excellence recognition
- **Purpose:** Third-party credibility.
- **Layout:** Single paragraph + link: "The Council for Advancement and Support of Education has awarded the Phillips Exeter Academy website with its Circle of Excellence Gold Medal. See the announcement here." Beige background, near-black text.

### Footer
Red background (solid). 4-column link hierarchy: "About Exeter", "Academics", "Student Life", "Just For You" + secondary columns "Admissions", "Alumni", "News", "More to Explore". Top of footer: 4 button-style CTA links ("Visit Exeter", "How to Apply", "Give", "Contact Us") in beige or white. Social icons (Facebook, X, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn) in near-white below main nav. Contact block (address, phone) bottom-left, beige text. Legal links (Terms of Use, Privacy Policy) bottom-center. Logo (lion, red) bottom-right.

**Notable patterns:**
- Feature images throughout use 1.35:1 ratio; thumbnail/card images are square (1:1) or mixed spans in bento.
- Bento grid in "Exeter Stories": 1 large story + 1 quote card + 2 smaller story cards, asymmetric layout.
- Stat cards ("Go figure") are uniform 4-column grid, centered.
- Alternation: Visit/Give section pairs large image left + text right on each side, symmetrical.
- Background: beige dominates (84.9% of page); red and olive accents in footer and minor elements.
- Rounded corners on buttons and cards (pills on CTA buttons); focus-visible states present.
