# Mohan Lab design system

## Product context

Mohan Lab is an academic biomedical research group at the University of Houston. The site must communicate scientific credibility, preserve the full audited legacy content, help collaborators understand research programs, and help prospective trainees discover opportunities including computational/AI tracks.

## Brand direction

Use an assertive institutional-editorial language rather than a startup or AI-template aesthetic. The result should feel rigorous, current, human, and distinctly tied to an established university research lab.

- Primary colors: ink `#17232d`, slate blue `#263c4b`, warm paper `#f6f5f2`, white `#ffffff`, UH red `#c8102e`.
- Secondary colors: soft ink `#3f4b54`, muted `#69737a`, deep paper `#ecebe7`, line `#d6d5cf`, dark red `#981027`.
- Typography: Georgia/Times editorial display for major headlines; Helvetica Neue/Helvetica/Arial for all navigation, labels, body copy, metadata, and controls.
- Geometry: square corners, visible rules, disciplined grids, dense-but-readable information hierarchy. Avoid floating pill cards, gradients, glassmorphism, generic blobs, excessive rounded corners, and decorative icons.
- Photography: real lab, people, project, and event imagery from the audited archive. Favor documentary crops and clear captions; do not invent synthetic scientific imagery.
- Density: desktop shell 1240px; page intros can be large, but subsequent content should begin promptly and maintain deliberate rhythm.
- Motion: restrained scroll reveals and image zoom only where it improves orientation. Respect reduced motion. No continuous decorative animation.
- Accessibility: body text at least 17px, controls at least 44px tall, high color contrast, visible focus, semantic headings, keyboard-ready navigation and disclosure controls.
- Responsive: collapse grids without losing hierarchy; contextual nav may scroll horizontally; avoid clipped copy and horizontal page overflow.

## Page system

- Global header: university utility strip above a clear Mohan Lab primary navigation.
- Page intros: eyebrow + serif headline + concise lead, followed by section navigation where useful.
- Core content: strong rules and asymmetric editorial grids, with UH red used sparingly for wayfinding and emphasis.
- Data/archives: headings and grouped disclosure patterns must remain usable for long publications, news, and source records.
- Footer: institutional statement, complete navigation, department, and address.

## Current refinement goal

Make the visual system feel tougher: deeper slate surfaces, sharper hierarchy, bolder section transitions, more decisive typography, more useful labels, and stronger editorial composition. Preserve all existing Mohan Lab content, real assets, accessibility, responsive behavior, and the restrained academic tone. The site must never feel like an AI-generated SaaS landing page.
